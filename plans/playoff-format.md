# The Playoffs

## 1. Playoff Eligibility (After GS4)

After Group Stage 4 concludes, the 16 teams with the highest Standings Points (SP) become **playoff eligible**.
Within this group of 16, there are two tiers:

- **Auto-Qualified (seeds 1–4):** The top 4 teams by SP are locked into the playoffs. These teams cannot be eliminated regardless of their GS5 performance.
- **At-Large (seeds 5–16):** The next 12 teams by SP. These teams must earn their playoff spot through GS5.

Teams ranked 17–32 by SP are eliminated from playoff contention but still participate in GS5 as part of the regular season schedule (their games affect promotion/relegation and SP for next season's starting position).

## 2. Group Stage 5 - Hybrid

GS5 serves a dual purpose: it is both the final regular-season stage **and** the play-in qualifier for the playoffs. Every team plays GS5 regardless of eligibility.

### Seeding the Play-In Groups

The 16 playoff-eligible teams are distributed into **4 play-in groups** using a snake draft of their SP seeds (1–16):

| Group | Seed 1 | Seed 2 | Seed 3 | Seed 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 1 | 8 | 9 | 16 |
| B | 2 | 7 | 10 | 15 |
| C | 3 | 6 | 11 | 14 |
| D | 4 | 5 | 12 | 13 |

This structure gives the highest seed the most favorable group draw: seed 1 plays seeds 8, 9, and 16, while seed 4 plays seeds 5, 12, and 13. Each group sums to the same total (34), but the highest seed always draws the lowest-seeded opponent within the pool.

### The Other Groups

The remaining 16 teams (seeds 17–32) are divided into 4 standard GS5 groups following the normal group-formation formula. These groups operate as regular-season games only - no playoff implications.

## 3. Elimination

Within each play-in group, the team with the **worst group record** is eliminated from playoff contention. However:

- **Auto-qualified teams (seeds 1–4) are protected.** If an auto-qualified team finishes with the worst record in its group, they are not eliminated. Instead, the elimination falls to the team with the worst record among the non-auto-qualified teams in that group.
- If two or more non-auto-qualified teams share the worst record, tiebreakers apply: GS5 Point Differential → Standings Points.

### Example

Group A contains seeds 1 (auto-qualified), 8, 9, and 16. Their GS5 results:

| Seed | Record | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 3–0 | Advances |
| 16 | 2–1 | Advances |
| 9 | 1–2 | Eliminated |
| 1 | 0–3 | Auto-qualified (protected) |

Seed 1 finished last but is protected. Elimination passes to seed 9 (1–2), the worst record among the remaining three teams. Seed 16 (2–1) and seed 8 (3–0) advance.

This produces a **12-team playoff field** - 4 teams eliminated (one per play-in group) from the 16 eligible.

## 4. Final Playoff Seeding

After GS5, the 12 advancing teams are re-seeded for the playoff bracket based on their **GS5 group performance only** (regular-season SP is not carried forward). Tiebreakers, in order:

1. **GS5 record** - teams with better records in their GS5 group place higher
2. **Point Differential** (PD) in GS5 games
3. **Standings Points** (SP) earned during GS5

This means a team that entered GS5 as the 12-seed can climb into a high playoff seed with a strong group performance, and vice versa - an auto-qualified team that stumbles through GS5 (e.g., 0–3) would enter the playoff bracket as a low seed despite their locked-in spot.

## 5. Playoff Bracket (12 Teams)

With 12 teams, the top 4 seeds receive a **first-round bye**. The bracket:

| Round 1 | Quarterfinals | Semifinals | Final |
|---|---|---|---|
| - | (1) Bye → | | |
| - | (2) Bye → | | |
| - | (3) Bye → | | |
| - | (4) Bye → | | |
| 5 vs 12 → | Winner vs 4 | | |
| 6 vs 11 → | Winner vs 3 | | |
| 7 vs 10 → | Winner vs 2 | | |
| 8 vs 9 → | Winner vs 1 | | |

All playoff rounds are single elimination. The higher seed hosts (or chooses home/away side). The final determines the season champion.

## Summary

```
Regular Season (GS1–GS4)
    │
    ├── Top 4 SP → AUTO-QUALIFIED (safe)
    ├── Next 12 SP → AT-LARGE (must earn spot)
    └── Remaining 16 → eliminated from playoffs
            │
            ▼
GS5 (Hybrid - all 32 teams play)
    │
    ├── 4 Play-In Groups (16 eligible teams, snake seeded)
    │   ├── Worst record eliminated from each group
    │   └── Auto-qualified teams are protected from elimination
    │
    └── 4 Regular Groups (17–32, standard format)
            │
            ▼
12-Team Playoff Bracket
    ├── Re-seeded by GS5 performance
    ├── Tiebreakers: GS5 Record → PD → SP
    └── Single elimination; top 4 seeds get Round 1 byes
```
