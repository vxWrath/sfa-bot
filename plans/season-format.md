- We will have 4 divisions of 8 teams each. Each division will be subdivided into two subdivisions, 'A' and 'B'. For example, the top 4 seeded teams will be in Division 1-A. I'm not set on the naming for this yet  if you have any suggestions, let me know.

## 2. Schedule

- Each team will play 12 regular season games each season. This will happen in a group format, where all teams will be set into groups of 4. These groups will be determined by initial seeding and then team performance, using a predetermined formula.

  > **TL;DR:** The schedule formula is **STATIC**, but since team seeding is **DYNAMIC**, the specific matchups will vary. This means no vibes-based schedule making  everything is predetermined.

- To fulfill the 12-game requirement, each team will be part of 4 groups each season. Repeat matchups may occur due to the limited number of teams, but if this does happen, it isn't necessarily a bad thing, because the teams will likely have a similar level of competitiveness (whether in the top or bottom levels of the standings).
- Our divisions will be fluid, meaning teams will ascend and descend through them based on their game results.
- In the past, I did standings based on point differential, which is a fine system and also what PFG is using currently (record first, then PD as a tiebreaker). However, I think a system more similar to LFG's Game Points is ideal and the most straightforward. I'll explain this in the next section.

## 3. Standings Points

- In the past, I did standings based on point differential, which is a fine system and also what PFG is using currently (record first, then PD as a tiebreaker). However, I think a system more similar to LFG's Game Points is ideal and the most straightforward.
- My idea is to have group standings determined the same as the current system  record first, then PD for tiebreakers  and then **Standings Points (SP)** will be assigned based on how teams finish within their groups, and what division they are currently in. The rundown of how SP will work is below.

### Base SP by Division

| Division | Win SP | Loss SP | Minimum SP from Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 15 | 8 | 24 |
| D2 | 12 | 6 | 18 |
| D3 | 9 | 4 | 12 |
| D4 | 6 | 3 | 9 |

### Bonus SP by Final Score Margin

How the final score turns out affects how much bonus SP is distributed on top of the base win/loss SP above.

| Point Differential From Game | Winning Team's Additional SP | Losing Team's Additional SP | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| (±) 0 | Scenario 1: +3<br>Scenario 2: +2<br>Scenario 3: +0 | Scenario 1: +0<br>Scenario 2: +2<br>Scenario 3: +0 | Scenario 1: FFW for the winning team / FFL for the losing team.<br>Scenario 2: results if a tie occurs (extremely rare, mainly a fallback).<br>Scenario 3: double FFL. |
| (±) 1–14 | +3 | +2 | |
| (±) 15–34 | +4 | +1 | |
| (±) 35+ | +6 | +0 | Mercy rule is set at 35. Teams may keep playing past this point, but no extra SP is awarded past +35. |

### Bonus SP by Group Standing Position (GSP)

On top of the above, teams will be awarded extra Standings Points based on their **Group Standing Position (GSP)** at the end of each Group Stage. (Remember: group standings are determined by record and point differential, **not** by Standings Points.) This bonus is the same across every division.

| Group Standing Position | Bonus SP |
|---|---|
| First Place (GSP 1) | +15 |
| Second Place (GSP 2) | +10 |
| Third Place (GSP 3) | +5 |
| Fourth Place (GSP 4) | +0 |

## 4. Promotion and Relegation

After each Group Stage is complete, teams will promote and relegate depending on their Group Standing Position (GSP) and which Stage it is. The tables below show the promotion/relegation outcome by Division and GSP.

### Group Stage 1

| Division | GSP 1 | GSP 2 | GSP 3 | GSP 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Stay | Demote | Demote | Double Demote |
| D2 | Promote | Promote | Demote | Double Demote |
| D3 | Double Promote | Promote | Stay | Demote |
| D4 | Double Promote | Promote | Stay | Stay |

### Group Stage 3

### Group Stage 4

| Division | GSP 1 | GSP 2 | GSP 3 | GSP 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Notes:** Group Stage 1 is the only one that differs from the rest. This is because at the beginning of the season, shuffling teams to increase parity between team performance and division placement is a major focus of this schedule system.

The purpose of Divisions is to let good teams earn extra SP while playing other good teams. **SP is the qualifying factor for the playoffs, not Division standing.**
